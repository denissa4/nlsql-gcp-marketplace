NLSQL AI Agent is a Microsoft Teams assistant that runs in your own AWS account. Employees ask questions in plain language and get answers grounded in your company documents, kept in your Amazon S3 bucket and answered by Claude on Amazon Bedrock. In DocsAndSql mode it also answers questions about your data from Microsoft SQL Server, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Amazon Redshift or Snowflake, with tables and charts. It deploys from one AWS CloudFormation template onto Amazon ECS with AWS Fargate, keeps credentials in AWS Secrets Manager, calls AWS services only through the task's IAM role, and keeps your documents and data in your account.

This product extends the functionality of Microsoft Teams and without it, this product has very limited utility. Please note that Microsoft Teams might require its own license for full functionality with this listing.
