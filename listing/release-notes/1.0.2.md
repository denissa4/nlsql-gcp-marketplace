Version title: 1.0.2 - Browser agent and image reading on your Bedrock model; Gemini optional

Release notes:

Critical
- None.

Important
- The browser agent (with its live view) and image reading no longer need Google Gemini. With GeminiFeatures=Disabled, the default, they run on your Claude model in Amazon Bedrock, through the task role, and nothing is sent to Google. In 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 they were offered only with a Google Gemini API key.
- With GeminiFeatures=Enabled they run on Google Gemini with your own key, and switch to your Claude model whenever Gemini fails: no prepaid credits or billing, an invalid or expired key, a quota used up, or a model that is gone. The answer still comes, and the log group gets one line, for example Browser agent: Gemini failed (ModelProviderError, status 429); the main model (<model>) goes on with the task. Before, such a failure ended the browser task and the image question with an error.
- Without Gemini, each step of a browser task is one Bedrock call with a screenshot of the page, billed to your account like any other Bedrock use.
- New parameter BrowserAgent (default Enabled). Disabled offers no browser agent, routes no live view and leaves out the BrowserViewUrl output; image reading stays on. Choose it if you want no browser in the task.
- With BrowserAgent=Enabled, the live browser's paths (/vnc/*, /websockify/*, /api/browser-use/*) and the BrowserViewUrl output are part of the stack whatever GeminiFeatures says. Teams packages built with teams/build-package.sh then include the Browser-Use help tab; rebuild and upload the package once if your stack had GeminiFeatures=Disabled.
- To upgrade, update the stack with this version's template, whose ImageUri default is the 1.0.2 image. The tables, buckets and secrets are kept, so users stay signed in with their history and tasks.

Known limitations
- Code mode (the coder service) is not part of 1.0.2.
- In Teams the app is installed in personal scope only, not in channels or group chats.
- A live-browser link sent outside Teams is valid for about an hour and works as a bearer credential; it is sent only into the user's own conversation.
- The service runs exactly one task: a deployment interrupts conversations in progress and ends live browser sessions.
